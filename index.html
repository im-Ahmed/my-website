01 / Perspective

There is a person
behind the process.

Engineering is what I'm learning to do. Curiosity, intention, and steady progress are how I try to do it.

I'd rather understand the thing than just learn how to make it work.

I'm studying Software Engineering at the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad, and working toward becoming a capable full-stack developer. I'm interested in how products fit together: the interface, the API, the data model, the decisions you only notice when something breaks.

I value fundamentals over shortcuts and honest learning over pretending. AI is a powerful tool, but I believe the engineer still needs to understand the system, the trade-offs, and the consequences of a decision. If I don't know something, I'd rather say so, learn it, and come back better prepared.

My taste outside code leans toward the understated: tailored silhouettes, dark tones, cinematic imagery, music that doesn't need words. I like design with restraint and character — not empty minimalism, but details that have a reason to be there.
